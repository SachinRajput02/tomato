Abstract Submission for V-Launchpad 2025 – Startup Ideathon Pitching




Problem Statement:
In many campuses, residential colonies, and small communities, food sellers struggle to create a digital presence for offering services to their local audience. Platforms like Swiggy or Zomato aren’t tailored for such hyper-local setups like college canteens or community kitchens, limiting the ability of small vendors to digitize and streamline their service.

Proposed Solution:
Tomato is a hyperlocal e-commerce platform that enables food sellers—such as college canteens or colony-based food providers—to create personalized online shops, interact with users in their specific region, and manage orders digitally. Users can browse nearby shops, place orders, and chat directly with sellers.

Technological Approach:
Built with HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB, Tomato includes secure payment integration, real-time order notifications using Firebase, and a Socket.io-powered chat system. We’ve also implemented separate login systems for users and sellers, and are currently integrating geolocation for local shop discovery. The full stack is deployed on Render for reliable performance.

Impact & Vision:
Tomato empowers local food providers to go digital without complexity. With modular tech and scalable deployment, our vision is to become the go-to platform for localized food commerce in institutions and communities across India.
